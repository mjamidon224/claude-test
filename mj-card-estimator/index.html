        PSA grading standards
        GradeFrontBackWhat it allows

        Centering is the share of the border on each side, larger side first. PSA tightened the PSA 10 front standard to 55/45 in 2025; older guides say 60/40. PSA also gives half grades from 1.5 to 8.5, and adds a qualifier (OC, ST, PD, OF, MK, MC) when one flaw is far worse than the rest. A qualified card usually sells closer to two grades lower.
